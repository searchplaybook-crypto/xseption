About X-SEPTION

European Craftsmanship. Local Style Authority.

Established in 1991, X-SEPTION is a premier South African menswear destination delivering an uncompromising luxury retail experience. For over three decades, we have curated collections for the discerning gentleman, offering a seamless bridge between world-class international fashion brands and local style prestige.

True style speaks every language. Whether you are navigating executive boardrooms in Sandton, making a statement at high-profile celebrations, or unwinding at weekend coastal retreats, our boutique offerings bring together high-end Euro-centric apparel, fine Italian leather footwear, exclusive limited drops and structured wardrobe tailoring.

Discover our curated formal wear, bespoke styling services and timeless European fits at our flagship locations: Nelson Mandela Square in Sandton (Shop 39) and Eastgate Shopping Centre (Shop U123).

06The counter

Questions, answered

Hours, the concierge and the houses on the floor. Anything else, ask on WhatsApp.

When are the boutiques open?

Both boutiques trade within their centre's hours. Today's hours, including public holidays, are kept current on each boutique's Google listing: Nelson Mandela Square and Eastgate.

Planning a special trip? Call Nelson Mandela Square on 010 226 9475 or Eastgate on 011 622 3511 first.

How does the WhatsApp concierge work?

Tap Ask about this under any Instagram post, choose your boutique and add your size. WhatsApp opens with the message already written, including the post link, and the team replies with what is on the floor at that boutique.

Enquiries from both boutiques go to one number, so nothing is lost between stores.

Which houses do you carry?
  • A Fish Named FredA playful, expressive menswear label whose visual identity uses colourful graphics and a fish emblem. Best suited to statement shirts, blazers, trousers and coordinated looks.
  • ALBERTOA European trousers and denim specialist suited to fit-led wardrobe foundations and polished casual bottoms.
  • Bruno BananiA German cult fashion and designer brand known for expressive underwear, nightwear, fragrances and statement casualwear.
  • bugattiA European smart-casual menswear label covering shirts, chinos, trousers, jeans, outerwear and related wardrobe staples.
  • Cesare PaciottiAn Italian footwear and accessories label associated with luxury fashion, Made-in-Italy craftsmanship and the signature dagger emblem.
  • Avenue George V ParisA statement-led Parisian casual wear label with graphic tops, coordinated casual pieces and bold visual motifs.
  • Jo GhostAn Italian footwear label with contemporary boots, shoes and textured leather styles, including distinctive tapered ankle boots.
  • MilestoneAn outerwear-focused European label known for seasonal jackets and leather-jacket styling.
  • Psycho BunnyA premium casualwear label associated with polos, shirts, caps and its recognisable bunny-and-crossbones emblem.
  • R2 AmsterdamA contemporary Dutch menswear label offering modern shirts, travel fabrics, herringbone textures and smart-casual staples.
  • Rock RevivalA premium denim label known for multiple jean fits, bold washes and decorative detailing with a rebellious identity.
  • VenturoKnown for delivering effortless confidence through custom-tailored cuts and meticulous fabric selection, the brand's new line brings minimalist aesthetics and exceptional comfort to your wardrobe.
  • XPOOOSA premium European sock and hosiery label known for innovative fabric blends, bold artistic prints and distinctive foot fashion.

Availability changes with each drop and can differ between the two boutiques.

Will a sold-out piece come back?

Not necessarily. Each drop is curated and finite, and restocks are not promised. When a piece is gone, it may not return, so ask early if something catches your eye.

How is the edit split between formal and casual?

Use the Casual and Formal switch above the shoe wall. Formal covers Smart Casual and Boardroom dressing. Casual covers Beach, Boardwalk and After Hours. It is one From Beach to Boardroom wardrobe, sorted by where you are headed.

Can I check stock before I visit?

Yes. Send a WhatsApp from any post on this page, or call Nelson Mandela Square on 010 226 9475 or Eastgate on 011 622 3511, and the team will confirm stock and sizes at your chosen boutique.

Where exactly are the boutiques?

Nelson Mandela Square: Shop 39, Sandton. Eastgate Shopping Centre: Shop U123, Upper Level, Bedfordview. Directions for both are just below.
